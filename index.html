What you’ll need

Based on verified official guidance (checked 19 Oct 2026). Not a complete legal checklist.

Who must register

  • Landlords of assured or regulated tenancies in England — register yourself and each let property.
  • Initially applies to properties under let (or that become occupied during rollout). Unoccupied / pre-marketing registration comes later under future rules.
  • Exempt: supported exempt accommodation (Supported Housing (Regulatory Oversight) Act 2023, s.12).
  • Joint landlords each make a landlord entry; they share one dwelling entry.

Sources: Housing Hub, Guide to the Renters’ Rights Act.

Documents & details

  • GOV.UK One Login
  • Your details (or organisation details if you’re a company/trust)
  • Property address, bedrooms, ownership/dwelling type, whether under let
  • Occupants / households, licence info (HMO / selective / additional) if relevant
  • Rent amount & frequency; furnished status
  • Gas safety record (if the property has a gas supply)
  • EICR (or appropriate EIC) and expiry date
  • EPC where you were required to give one to the current tenant (plus MEES exemption info if below minimum)

Source: Housing Hub – information you’ll be asked to provide. Deposit protection is a separate legal duty — it is not listed as a database registration upload on that page.

Fee

  • £65 per property per year, renewed annually. During the regional rollout the fee is pro-rated so early registrants don’t pay more.

Source: Housing Hub (the SI lets the database operator set the fee; £65 is stated on Housing Hub).

Agents

  • You (the landlord) must start the registration. An agent or property manager may upload certain information on your behalf. You remain responsible for the required information.

Source: Housing Hub FAQ.

Penalties if you don’t register

  • Civil penalty up to £7,000 for letting or advertising without registration.
  • Up to £40,000 (or criminal prosecution) for repeated breaches or serious offences such as fraudulent information.
  • While in breach you generally cannot get a possession order (except anti-social behaviour grounds 7A / 14).
  • Rent repayment orders can apply for continued failure after a penalty, or for false information to the database (up to 24 months’ rent).

Source: Guide to the Renters’ Rights Act – PRS Database.

Frequently asked questions

When does the London registration window open?

London’s window opens on 15 July 2027 and the deadline to register is 14 October 2027 (Housing Hub / PRS Database Regulations 2026).

When does registration start in England?

The service launches on 15 December 2026, starting in the West Midlands, then rolls out region by region. All landlords actively letting property should be registered by 14 November 2027.

How much is the registration fee?

£65 per property per year, renewed annually. During the regional rollout the fee is pro-rated so early registrants don’t pay more (Housing Hub).

Can a letting agent register for me?

You (the landlord) must start the registration. An agent or property manager may upload certain information on your behalf; you remain responsible for the required information (Housing Hub).
